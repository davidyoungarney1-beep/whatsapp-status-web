📱 How to Use the WhatsApp Status Forwarder

Follow these steps carefully to make sure your WhatsApp Status is forwarded to the groups you choose.

1. Connect Your WhatsApp

  1. Enter your WhatsApp number and click Connect WhatsApp.
  2. A pairing code will appear. Enter the code in WhatsApp through:

    WhatsApp → Linked Devices → Link a Device → Link with phone number
  3. After entering the pairing code, wait a little while on WhatsApp for the account to finish connecting. Do not close WhatsApp immediately.
  4. Wait until the website shows that your WhatsApp account is Connected.

2. Turn Forwarding ON

After your account connects, make sure the Forwarding switch is turned ON.

3. Load Your Groups

  1. Click Load Groups under your connected WhatsApp account.
  2. Wait for your WhatsApp groups to appear.
  3. Select the groups where you want your WhatsApp Status to be posted.

4. IMPORTANT — Click Save Groups

⚠️ Always click "Save Groups" after choosing your groups.

Whenever you want to post a new status, make sure you select the group or groups you want to use and then click Save Groups again.

After the groups are successfully saved, the forwarding status should return to Waiting.

5. Keep Your Personal Status Private

Before posting your WhatsApp Status, go to:

WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy → Status

Choose: Only share with...

Then select ONLY ONE USER.

This means your normal WhatsApp friends and other contacts will not see the status. The bot will detect your status and forward it to the groups you selected.

6. Post Your Status

Now post your image, video, or text as a normal WhatsApp Status.

7. Watch the Forwarding Status

Waiting
The bot is ready and waiting for your next status.

Forwarding
Your status has been detected and the bot has started forwarding it to your selected groups.

Forwarded
The status has successfully been forwarded to the selected groups.

Partially Forwarded
The status reached some of the selected groups but not all.

Failed
The bot was unable to forward the status to the selected groups.

🔁 For Every New Status

Whenever you want to post another status:

  1. Choose the group or groups you want to receive it.
  2. Click Save Groups.
  3. Make sure the status shows Waiting.
  4. Post your WhatsApp Status while sharing it with ONLY ONE USER.
  5. Once the bot detects it, the status will change to Forwarding.
  6. Wait for it to show Forwarded.

Always make sure Forwarding is ON, your desired groups are selected, and you have clicked Save Groups before posting.
