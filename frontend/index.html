How to Use the WhatsApp Status Forwarder

Connect WhatsApp, choose your destination groups, save your selection, and then post. Keep this page open while the bot is working.

1. Connect WhatsApp

  1. Enter your WhatsApp number in international format without the plus sign (for example, 2348012345678) and select Use Pairing Code, or choose Scan QR Code Instead.
  2. For a pairing code, open WhatsApp on your phone and go to Linked devices → Link a device → Link with phone number. Enter the code shown on this page. For QR, scan the displayed code from Linked devices.
  3. Keep WhatsApp open until this page shows Connected. If it disconnects, reconnect the account.

2. Choose how to post

  • Website Direct (Text + Link): Write your status text and include your link in the same box, then choose Post Text to My Status. This mode is for text and links only; it does not post photos or videos.
  • Manual WhatsApp Status: Open WhatsApp on your phone and publish your text, photo, or video as a normal Status. With forwarding enabled, the bot will attempt to forward it to your saved groups.

3. Select destination groups

  1. Under the connected account, click Load Groups and wait for your groups to appear.
  2. Tick the groups that should receive your status. Use Select All Groups or Clear Selection if helpful.
  3. The small ⌃ / ⌄ button beside the Groups heading hides or shows the group list. It does not change your selections.
  4. Click Save Groups and wait for the save to finish. Saving stores your forwarding selection; it does not join groups.
Before posting: Make sure the account says Connected, the Forwarding switch is ON, your intended groups are selected, and you have clicked Save Groups.

4. Post and check progress

Use Website Direct for text plus a link, or Manual WhatsApp Status for photos and videos. Watch the live status on the account card:

Waiting
The bot is ready for a new status.

Forwarding
The bot detected a status and is attempting to send it to the selected groups.

Forwarded
The forwarding task completed successfully for the selected destinations.

Partially Forwarded
Some destinations may not have received the status. Check the progress details and group availability.

Failed
The bot could not complete forwarding. Check the connection, enable forwarding, save your group selection again if needed, and retry.

5. For each new status

  1. Check that forwarding is ON.
  2. Review your selected groups and click Save Groups if you changed them.
  3. Post through the mode you want to use.
  4. Keep this page open and wait for the progress status to finish.

Privacy reminder: WhatsApp Status privacy settings control who can see the original status. Review those settings before posting; forwarding may send the content to the groups you selected.
